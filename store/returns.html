What can be returned

  • Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all tags, accessories and the invoice.
  • Damaged, defective or wrong items can always be returned or replaced. Please tell us within 48 hours of delivery and share a photo.
  • For hygiene reasons, opened groceries and opened beauty products can’t be returned unless they arrived damaged or wrong.

How to return an item

  1. Message us on WhatsApp at (or use the Contact page) with your order ID and the item you want to return.
  2. We’ll confirm the return and arrange a pickup from your address, usually within 2–3 working days.
  3. Once the item reaches us and passes a quick check, we process your refund or exchange.

Refunds

  • Paid online (UPI or card): refunded to the same payment method within 5–7 working days after we receive the item.
  • Cash on Delivery: refunded to your bank account or UPI ID within 5–7 working days after we receive the item. We’ll ask you for the details.
  • Delivery charges are refunded if the return is because of our mistake (damaged, defective or wrong item).
  • If you used a coupon, the refund is the amount you actually paid.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel free of charge before your order is shipped. Message us on WhatsApp with your order ID. Once an order has shipped, you can refuse it at delivery or return it under this policy.

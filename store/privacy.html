Privacy policy

Your information, handled with care.

Last updated:

This policy explains what personal information (operated by ) collects, why, and the choices you have. We follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and related rules.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, mobile number, delivery address, your email (if you give it) and the items you order, so we can deliver your order, send you a receipt and contact you about it.
  • Messages: anything you send us on WhatsApp or email, such as questions or return requests.
  • Payment details: we never store your card number, CVV or UPI PIN. When online payments are enabled, they’re handled by a secure payment provider.

What stays on your device

To make shopping easier, some information is saved only in your own browser and isn’t sent to us: your cart, wishlist, recently viewed items, order history, preferences (like dark mode or Easy mode) and, only if you choose “Save this address”, your delivery address. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data, or tap “Forget this address” at checkout.

How we use your information

  • To process, deliver and support your orders, returns and refunds.
  • To reply to your questions and complaints.
  • To meet legal requirements, such as tax and accounting records.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising tracking.

Who we share it with

  • Delivery partners: your name, phone number and address, so they can deliver your order.
  • Google: order details are stored in our Google account (Google Sheets) and receipts are sent by Gmail, so we can manage orders and email you.
  • WhatsApp and SMS: when you message us or send an order on WhatsApp or SMS, the message is handled under WhatsApp’s or your mobile network’s own policies.
  • Service providers: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Voice search uses your browser’s speech service, which may send audio to your browser’s provider while you speak.
  • Authorities: only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order information for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal and tax obligations, then delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it (unless we must keep it by law). You can also withdraw your consent at any time. To do this, contact our grievance officer below.

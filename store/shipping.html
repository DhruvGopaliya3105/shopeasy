If we can’t deliver

Our delivery partner will try to contact you if they can’t find the address or nobody is available. Please make sure your phone number and address are correct. If an order can’t be delivered after repeated attempts, it’s returned to us and any amount paid is refunded as described in our Returns & refunds policy.

Where we deliver

We deliver to most PIN codes in India. If we can’t deliver to your PIN code, we’ll let you know and refund any amount paid.

    Frequently asked questions

    How long does delivery take?

    Groceries arrive in 1–2 days. Most other items arrive in 3–5 days. Every product page shows the expected delivery date before you buy.

    Is delivery free?

    Yes, on orders above ₹999. Below that, delivery costs ₹49. You can also use the coupon FREESHIP for free delivery on any order.

    Can I pay Cash on Delivery?

    Yes. Choose “Cash on Delivery” at checkout and pay by cash or UPI when your order arrives.

    How do I return an item?

    Most items can be returned within 7 days of delivery (fashion within 10 days). Opened groceries can't be returned. Message us on WhatsApp with your order ID and we'll arrange a pickup.

    Is it safe to pay online?

    You can pay by UPI, card or Cash on Delivery. We never store your card or UPI details on this site.

    How can I track my order?

    Tap 📦 My Orders to see every order you've placed and where it is: Ordered, Packed, Shipped, Out for delivery or Delivered.

    How do I use a coupon?

    Open your cart, type the code in the Coupons box (or tap “Apply” next to one of the listed coupons), and the discount is taken off your total straight away.

    Can I talk to someone?

    Yes! Tap Ask ShopEasy at the bottom right to chat with our shopping assistant by text or voice, or choose Talk to a person there to message our team on WhatsApp.

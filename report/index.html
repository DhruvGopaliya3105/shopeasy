Summary

Where the project stands

What's done DONE

  • A complete, professional-looking store (premium navy and gold design) that works on phones and computers, with a dark mode.
  • Full shopping flow: search, product pages, cart, coupons, checkout, order confirmation and order tracking.
  • Features made for Indian customers: Hinglish search, price bargaining (mol-bhav), Cash on Delivery, a simple Easy mode with Hindi labels.
  • Brand tagline "Quality you can trust. Prices you'll love." and 26 festival themes (Guru Nanak Jayanti, Christmas, Holi, Eid, Raksha Bandhan, Navratri, Diwali and more) that switch on automatically on the right dates.
  • Orders reach the owner on WhatsApp (+91 99501 99227) or SMS, and customers get their receipt on their own mobile number.
  • About, Contact, Shipping, Returns, Privacy and Terms pages, plus a go-live checklist.
  • Tested in the browser at phone and desktop sizes; bugs found during testing were fixed.

Still needed before launch TO DO

  • Real payments. UPI and card are in test mode: the screen says "Payment successful" but no money is collected. Needs a Razorpay account (business KYC).
  • Order records. Orders reach the owner only when the customer taps the WhatsApp or SMS button. There is no order database yet.
  • Real product photos (emoji are placeholders) and real prices.
  • Business details (legal name, address, GSTIN, grievance officer) and a legal review of the policy pages.
  • Hosting on a real web address (free on Netlify).

Brand and festival offers

Tagline and festival calendar

The store has a clear brand line and changes its look for every major festival, with no manual work.

Tagline

Quality you can trust. Prices you'll love.

Shown on the homepage, browser tab and WhatsApp share preview. It speaks to the two things every shopper cares about, and the store backs both up: ratings, quality-checked products and easy returns, plus real discounts, coupons and Deal of the Day.

How festival mode works

  • Turns on automatically a few days before each festival and ends the day after.
  • Each festival has its own colours, headline, a "days to go" countdown, matching gift picks and a greeting for the gift message (for example "Eid Mubarak!").
  • When two festivals overlap, the one coming up soonest is shown.
  • Guru Nanak Jayanti (24 Nov) is live on the store today.
  • These are themes and gift picks, not discounts. Festival coupon codes can be added next.

How it works today

From order to delivery

There is no server or database yet, so orders travel through WhatsApp or SMS, started by a tap from the customer.

CustomerPlaces the orderCart, address, payment method.
CustomerTaps WhatsApp or SMSOrder is pre-written to the store's number.
OwnerReceives the orderItems, total, name, phone, address.
OwnerConfirms in one tapLink opens that customer's chat with a confirmation.
OwnerPacks and deliversCollects cash or UPI on delivery.

Work done

What was built, in order

  1. Core shopping features

    Buy Now, Undo after removing an item, Save for later, search suggestions, typo-tolerant search and voice search.

  2. Bigger catalogue

    Grew from 31 to 71 products, with at least 8 in every category.

  3. Product pages, wishlist and coupons

    A page and link for every product, wishlist, coupon codes (FIRST10, SAVE100, FREESHIP), Deal of the Day, Recently viewed, price and rating filters, free-delivery progress bar, trust badges, WhatsApp sharing and link previews.

  4. Customer-friendly features

    Phone bottom menu, Back to top, saved address, My Orders with tracking and Buy again, FAQ section and a WhatsApp chat button.

  5. Smart features for Indian shoppers

    Mol-bhav bargaining, Hinglish search, festival mode, Budget Basket, "I'm going to…" kits, monthly ration reminder, swipe to discover, savings piggy bank, Easy mode and shared cart links.

  6. Professional redesign

    Premium navy and gold look, serif headings, line icons instead of emoji in menus, and a new tagline: "Quality you can trust. Prices you'll love."

  7. Homepage polish

    Offer bar, logo and browser-tab icon, hero product collage, category picture tiles, promo banners, Shop by price, honest badges, curated rows, Show more, fly-to-cart animation and a sticky buy bar on phones.

  8. Go-live preparation

    "Payment successful" screen (test mode), orders sent to the owner on WhatsApp, product photo support, six policy and contact pages, a Cash-on-Delivery-only switch and a go-live checklist.

  9. Receipts on the customer's own number

    Receipts by WhatsApp or SMS to whatever number the customer enters, plus an SMS option for customers without WhatsApp. An automatic email receipt feature was also built and tested, then removed at the owner's request.

  10. Handover for future work

    Project opened in VS Code with the Claude Code extension installed, and a beginner's guide to using it.

  11. Full festival calendar

    26 festival themes from November 2026 to December 2027, each with its own colours, headline, gift picks and greeting, with dates checked online. Festival pages were made easier to read on warm festival colours.

Quality

Testing and deliverables

How it was tested

  • Every feature was used in a real browser at phone and desktop sizes, in light and dark mode.
  • Prices, coupons, delivery charges and totals were checked by calculation (for example ₹1,499 + 2 × ₹199 − ₹190 coupon = ₹1,707).
  • WhatsApp and SMS messages were checked for different customer numbers, and for Android and iPhone link formats.
  • Bugs found during testing and fixed included: a picture helper that froze the page, a bargain section missing on shared links, the toast covering the checkout button, and layout issues on small phones.

Not tested: real payments, delivery partner integration, and the store running on a public web address.
